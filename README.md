# ✨ My Calendar - Student Life Organizer

A personalized, aesthetic calendar app designed to help busy students balance academics, extracurriculars, work, and social life.

![Calendar Preview](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-Live-success?style=for-the-badge)
![Grade 12 CLC Project](https://img.shields.io/badge/Project-Grade%2012%20CLC-pink?style=for-the-badge)

---

## 🎯 The Problem

As a Grade 12 student juggling:
- ⚽ High-level competitive soccer (including missing school for nationals)
- 💼 Part-time work
- 🤝 Library volunteering  
- 📚 Demanding academics
- 🎓 University applications
- ❤️ Maintaining a social life

...I struggled to find a calendar that was both **functional** and **reflected my personality**. Most calendars are boring, overwhelming, or don't help visualize balance across different life areas.

---

## 💡 The Solution

**My Calendar** is a fun, customizable web app that:
- Makes scheduling feel less like a chore
- Helps visualize time across different categories
- Stays organized without sacrificing personality
- Works beautifully on any device

---

## ✨ Features

### 📅 **Smart Calendar Management**
- Month, week, and day views
- Click any day to add events instantly
- Recurring events (daily, weekly, bi-weekly, monthly)
- All-day event support
- Quick week strip for easy navigation

### 🎨 **Personalization**
- **6 Categories**: Soccer, School, Work, Social, Volunteering, Personal
- **Custom themes**: Choose between Soft & Pastel or Bright & Fun color modes
- **6 color schemes** per mode
- **Background wallpapers**: Polka dots, stripes, hearts, or none
- **50+ emojis** to represent different activities
- **Custom alerts**: Set reminders from 5 minutes to 1 day before events

### 💾 **Automatic Saving**
- All data saves automatically to your browser
- Events persist even after closing the tab
- No account required
- Works completely offline

### 🎯 **Category Filtering**
- Toggle categories on/off to focus on specific areas
- Color-coded events for quick visual scanning
- Balance your time across life priorities

- **React** - Component-based UI

- **localStorage** - Persistent data storage

---

## 🚀 How It Works

1. **Add Events**: Click any day or the "Add Event" button
2. **Choose Category**: Soccer, School, Work, Social, Volunteering, or Personal
3. **Pick an Emoji**: 50+ options organized by type
4. **Set Details**: Title, date, time, duration, notes
5. **Customize Alerts**: Choose when you want to be reminded
6. **Repeat if Needed**: Set up recurring events for regular activities
7. **Save Automatically**: Everything saves instantly!
